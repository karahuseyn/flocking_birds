Octonion token stream (mixed & quantized)


  







  Each 8-byte block becomes an octonion O. It is rotated into a transform basis
  by a unit key K (octonion product P = O⊗K, norm-preserving), and
  the 8 components of P are uniformly quantized over a shared range. Decoding
  dequantizes and applies the exact octonion inverse P⊗K⁻¹ = O — valid by
  Artin's theorem, since O and K generate an associative subalgebra.
  The rotation mixes all 8 bytes together, so fidelity is best read from the byte RMSE rather
  than exact-character accuracy.
